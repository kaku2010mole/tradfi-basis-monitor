Data sources & methodology

Price, NAV & hedge ratios

Jupiter Price v3 supplies a transaction-based USD reference price. Pool NAV and custody balances come from Jupiter pool data. Theoretical hedge ratios use net custody delta, including Jupiter traders' positions, rather than spot asset weights.

For asset i, Aᵢ = Gᵢ + (ownedᵢ − lockedᵢ)Pᵢ + Sᵢ(Pᵢ / P̄ᵢ − 1). Thus hedge units per JLP = [(ownedᵢ − lockedᵢ) + Sᵢ / P̄ᵢ] / JLP supply; NAV hedge ratio = (Aᵢ − Gᵢ + Sᵢ) / pool AUM. A is custody AUM, G is guaranteed USD and S is trader short size. See official custody fields.

Leveraged neutral carry

JLP equity E buys L × E of JLP at market price. Debt is (L − 1) × E, so 1× has no borrowing interest. All JLP exposure is hedged on Hyperliquid, including the first 1×. Negative funding reduces carry. HL margin M equals hedge notional / hedge leverage K. Total capital T = E + M; for a fixed T, E = T / (1 + L × H / K), where H is the total absolute HL mark-valued hedge notional per $1 of JLP.

Net carry on JLP equity R = L × [JLP APR × NAV / market price + Σ(units per JLP × HL oracle / market price × funding APR)] − (L − 1) × borrow APR. Total-capital APR = R / (1 + L × H / K). Funding is valued at the HL oracle price. This is simple annualized carry on JLP equity plus HL initial margin. Margin assumes leverage K on the short hedge and no extra reserve; actual requirements and liquidation buffers may differ.

Assumes unchanged rates and continuous rebalancing. Excludes execution fees, slippage, price/NAV convergence, hedge basis changes, residual trader PnL and margin opportunity cost. It is a theoretical carry estimate, not realized PnL or a guarantee of neutrality.

Yield, funding & history

Current JLP APR/APY follows Jupiter's published update cycle. OpenJUP supplies completed daily observations of rolling 7-day fee APR; it remains separate from the official series. Borrow APR is from Jupiter JLP Loan.

Hyperliquid supplies current and settled funding. Annualized funding = hourly rate × 8,760. Hedge and carry histories start when this feature was enabled; they use contemporaneous snapshots, never today's ratios applied to old rates. Local samples are written once per minute; live values refresh every 10–15s. Samples pause during sleep or shutdown. Times are HKT; CSV timestamps are UTC.
